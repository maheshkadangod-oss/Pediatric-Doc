03Surgery day

You'll knowwhat happensbefore it does.

The hardest part for most parents is the unknown. So here's the whole day, in the order it happens.

1
Two weeks before

The first visit

I examine your child, explain the problem with simple drawings, and tell you honestly whether surgery is needed at all.

2
The night before

A story for your child

You get a one-page fasting guide and a short picture story your child can read about their own hospital day.

3
The morning

The operation

You stay with your child until they're asleep. Their favourite toy comes with them. I come out to see you the moment we finish.

4
Afterwards

Going home

A written recovery plan, a direct number for questions, and a follow-up visit about a week later.

One parent stays, always.Overnight in every case, in the same room as your child.
