Surgery day, step by step

You'll know what happens before it happens.

The scariest part for most parents is the unknown. So here's the whole day, laid out.

1

The first visit

I examine your child, explain the problem with drawings, and tell you honestly whether surgery is needed at all.

2

The night before

You get a short fasting guide and a picture story your child can read about their own hospital day.

3

The operation

You stay with your child until they're asleep. I come out to see you the moment we finish.

4

Going home

A written recovery plan, a direct number for questions, and a follow-up visit about a week later.

One parent can stay overnight in every case. Bring their favourite toy. It's welcome in the operating room until they fall asleep.
