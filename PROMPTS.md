# Image and video prompts

Save each file in an `assets/` folder next to `index.html`, with the exact filename below. The site swaps the placeholder for your file on its own. No code changes needed.

Works in Google AI Studio (Imagen / Veo), Midjourney, or any image/video tool.

**Style line to paste at the end of every image prompt, so the set looks like one shoot:**

> Warm natural window light, soft cream and pale sky-blue palette with small touches of coral and mustard yellow, shot on 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, editorial healthcare photography, calm and reassuring mood, no text, no logos.

### VID-02 · Meet the doctor (the main film)
`assets/vid-02-meet.mp4` · 16:9 · 60 seconds

Shoot this one for real with the doctor. AI video of a real doctor talking will look fake, and parents will notice. Use AI only for the B-roll shots.

**Storyboard**

| Time | Shot | What she says |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 6s | Close-up, she looks up from a child's drawing and smiles | "The night before surgery, parents usually ask me the same thing." |
| 6 to 18s | Medium shot, seated in the consult room | "Will my child be in pain. Will they be scared. Will I be allowed in." |
| 18 to 34s | B-roll: corridor, toy on a bed, hands holding hands | "Here's what I tell them. You'll stay until they fall asleep. I'll come to you the moment we're done." |
| 34 to 50s | Back to her, direct to camera | "Most children are home the same day or the next. The scar is smaller than you're picturing." |
| 50 to 60s | Wide shot, she walks down the corridor, title card | "If you have questions, bring them. I'd rather answer ten than leave one." |

**B-roll prompt (AI)**

> Slow handheld shot down a bright children's hospital corridor, pastel walls with hand-drawn animal murals, soft daylight, a small wheelchair with a teddy bear in it, shallow depth of field, calm cinematic mood, 24fps.
